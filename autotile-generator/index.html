Autotile generator

Paint terrain while the transitions take care of themselves.

All Tiled Up generates autotile-ready terrain and lets you test it immediately in a map. It is built for developers who want to spend less time hand-placing edge and corner tiles.

Try it in the browserRead the source

What is an autotile generator? An autotile generator chooses the correct edge, corner, or transition tile from neighboring terrain as you paint. This keeps borders consistent across rivers, coastlines, cliffs, and biome changes.

Supported autotile workflows

Wang tiles

Generate and paint edge-matching Wang transitions from blended materials, then verify the resulting seams in context.

Blob and Corner formats

Work with common blob and corner transition layouts instead of manually sorting every tile in a spritesheet.

Auto-detect existing atlases

Upload an existing tile atlas and use the detector to identify supported autotiling formats from the spritesheet.

Layered terrain

Paint multiple terrain layers with ramps, edge shadows, directional lighting, caves, stamps, and prefabs.

From transition tiles to a playable map

The useful test is not whether an atlas looks organized. It is whether the transitions hold up in a real map. Paint rivers, coastlines, cliffs, and biome edges, then verify how the rules behave before export.
